OneHand Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026
OneHand does not send the student information you record to any server. Student names, observation notes, and audio recordings are all stored only on your device, and no one, including the developer, can view them.

1. Information stored only on your device

All of the following information created in the app is kept only in your device’s internal storage (the app’s own database and files).

• Class roster information, such as class names and types, and student numbers and names

• The text, tags, and date and time of observation notes

• Recorded audio files and speech recognition results

This information is not transmitted outside your device, and the developer cannot access it by any means. If you delete the app, this information is also deleted from your device.

2. How speech recognition works

Recorded speech is converted to text using only the on-device speech recognition of iOS and Android. The app is configured to require on-device recognition, so your voice is not sent outside your device, including to Apple or Google servers.

To improve recognition accuracy, the list of student names in the current class is passed to the on-device recognizer as reference information, but this information does not leave your device either.

3. Microphone permission

The app requests microphone and speech recognition permissions only when you use the voice recording feature. Recording takes place only when you start it yourself, and the app never records in the background on its own.

4. When the app communicates externally

The app uses the network only in the following two cases, and neither includes any student information.

• In-app purchase (Pro) processing: The app communicates with the RevenueCat service to check your purchase status. Your purchase history and an anonymous identifier automatically generated by the app are sent at this time; no information that identifies you personally, such as your name or email address, is sent.

• Checking for app updates: To keep the app’s screens up to date, the app downloads update files from the Expo update server. Notes stored on your device are not transmitted.

On Android, if the on-device speech recognition model for your language is not installed, the app may ask the device’s speech service (Google) to download it. The download is handled by the operating system and contains no student information.

The app does not use advertising or any tools that analyze or track user behavior.

5. Backup files

The backup feature, which you run yourself, creates an encrypted file of your notes and exports it to a location you choose (such as the Files app or another app). Only you know the password, and the developer cannot recover it. You are responsible for storing and managing exported files.

The export features (copying text, PDF, Excel) also deliver data only at the time you choose and only to the destination you specify.

6. About student information

This app is a tool for teachers to record observations of students for work purposes. It is not an app for students to use, and it does not collect information directly from students.

Recorded student information does not leave your device, but you, as the user (teacher), are responsible for following the privacy guidelines of your school and education authority. In case your device is lost, we recommend using a device lock and the app lock (Face ID, fingerprint, etc.).

The app includes features to completely delete a class and to archive notes at the end of the school year, so you can clean up notes whose retention period has ended.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will inform you of the updated content along with an app update.
